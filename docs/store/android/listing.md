# Google Play listing draft

## Candidate public copy

**App name:** Flint: Peak Focus

**Short description:** Make room for focus with local app blocks, schedules and daily limits.

**Full description:**

Make room for the things you choose. Flint helps you set boundaries around
distracting apps with focus sessions, recurring schedules and daily limits.
Create groups of apps and choose the rules that fit your day.

Flint works locally on your phone. No account is required. The release app has
no analytics, ads or cloud sync. Your rules and preferences stay on your device.
All protection levels are free.

On Android, Flint uses Accessibility access to identify the foreground app and
read the address bar in supported browsers so your selected app and website
rules can show a blocking screen. This information is processed locally and is
not sent to Flint or third parties. Accessibility is optional; Usage Access and
display-over-other-apps access provide a more limited app-blocking fallback.
Website rules require Accessibility and a supported, readable browser address
bar. Usage Access also supports local usage summaries and daily app limits.

You control special access in Android Settings. Android and device-maker power
settings can affect background protection. Flint is a personal focus aid; it
does not promise to make your phone tamper-proof or guarantee uninterrupted
blocking on every device.
