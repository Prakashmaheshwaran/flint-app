## Finding details

### R-01 — PIN blocks minimal recovery and Emergency Pass

**P0 · iOS · PC-50.** Sources: [FlintApp.swift:41](https://github.com/Prakashmaheshwaran/flint-app/blob/2b91849534e757952c80662ddb90235129aaa8b9/ios-app/Flint/FlintApp.swift#L41); [AppLockWindow.swift:132](https://github.com/Prakashmaheshwaran/flint-app/blob/2b91849534e757952c80662ddb90235129aaa8b9/ios-app/Flint/AppLockWindow.swift#L132); [FlintApp.swift:147](https://github.com/Prakashmaheshwaran/flint-app/blob/2b91849534e757952c80662ddb90235129aaa8b9/ios-app/Flint/FlintApp.swift#L147).

**Reproduce:** Enable the app PIN, start Hardcore, then background and reopen Flint. The source window is hidden, inaccessible and noninteractive; the modal gate offers only PIN and Unlock. Pass/recovery cannot be reached without the PIN.

**Fix direction:** Expose a minimal recovery/pass view from the locked gate while keeping private content inaccessible.

**Follow-up:** PR #152 at dd60684516a001087d8b6aac242ab8582af36f46 source-closes the singleton PIN-route barrier, while rule-only/global recovery remains incomplete; see the follow-up table.

### R-02 — Active rule, Sleep and limit edits bypass current-tier authority

**P0 · Android · PC-10, PC-11, PC-12, PC-14, PC-15, PC-16, PC-18, PC-27, PC-56, PC-79, PC-82, PC-83.** Sources: [BlocklistViewModel.kt:181](https://github.com/Prakashmaheshwaran/flint-app/blob/2b91849534e757952c80662ddb90235129aaa8b9/android-app/feature/feature-blocklist/src/main/kotlin/com/flint/peakfocus/feature/blocklist/BlocklistViewModel.kt#L181); [DataStoreStores.kt:54](https://github.com/Prakashmaheshwaran/flint-app/blob/2b91849534e757952c80662ddb90235129aaa8b9/android-app/core/core-datastore/src/main/kotlin/com/flint/peakfocus/core/datastore/DataStoreStores.kt#L54).

**Follow-up:** Source-closed for the reviewed new-cause UI routes by PR #139 head 9b43f63 (merged as 97b7662 after the baseline). Detector/time/pass integration remains separate; see R-18 and R-34.
